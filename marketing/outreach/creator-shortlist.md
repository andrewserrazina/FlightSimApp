## Contact workflow
1. Verify published contact method, current posting activity and airliner relevance; manually assess at least 3 recent videos.
2. Score 1–5 for audience match, detailed airliner content, audience trust, ease of contact, and likelihood of genuinely using the product. High follower count alone is not a reason for high priority.
3. Confirm Windows / MSFS 2020 or 2024 experience, usage of SimBrief/GSX, and willingness to report bugs.
4. Send a single individualized outreach note *after* a real demo exists; no mass unsolicited emails.
5. Follow up at most once after 7–10 business days if appropriate. Respect declines and opt-outs.
6. Record any access, compensation or disclosure requirements. Encourage independent critical feedback.

## Initial outreach note (private feedback, not an endorsement)
**Subject:** Could I get your feedback on an MSFS airline-operations app?

Hi [Creator],

I'm building Turnline, a pilot-first airline operations companion for MSFS. The goal is to connect flight dispatch, passenger and ground operations, and the debrief into one consistent experience rather than a collection of disconnected add-ons.

We're still developing and testing it, so I'm not looking for a promotional video or positive review. Once the Windows build has been validated, I'd be interested in showing you a short demo and hearing what you think would make the experience useful for the kind of flying you do.

Would a 15–20 minute feedback session be of interest?

Thanks,
The Turnline team

## Before influencer promotion
Do not represent beta access as compensation-free if it has value; use clear disclosure requirements, respect creator independence, and track any paid arrangements. Never solicit public claims about integrations that haven't been tested.
